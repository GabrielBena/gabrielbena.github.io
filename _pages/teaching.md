## Imperial College London

**Neuroscience and Artificial Intelligence** — co-taught with Prof. Dan Goodman in the
Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. A course on what the two fields have
to say to each other: what neural computation actually buys you, and where the analogy
between brains and networks stops being useful.

**Data Science** — Imperial College Business School.

## Supervision

I have supervised MSc students at Imperial College London and, since moving to Zurich, at
the Institute of Neuroinformatics, on projects spanning neural cellular automata,
self-organising circuits, and differentiable compilation for neuromorphic substrates.

## Open science

I contributed to [COMOB](https://comob-project.github.io/snn-sound-localization/), a
grassroots, massively collaborative computational neuroscience project which brought
together 31 researchers and resulted in a peer-reviewed publication — an experiment in
what open, distributed science can look like, and a genuinely good way to learn by doing.
